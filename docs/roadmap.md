- Repository setup.
- MVP scope lock.

## Phase 4: Communication

- Meeting notes.
- Action items.
- TRTC / CallKit proof of concept.

## Phase 5: Growth and Monetization

- Family annual memory generation.
- Membership tiers.
- Larger storage packages.
- Advanced reports and templates.
- Content acquisition through official account and video channel.
